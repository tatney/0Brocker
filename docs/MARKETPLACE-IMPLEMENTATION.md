## Historical implementation record - 13 October 2026

Implemented 13 October 2026. The decisions are recorded in MARKETPLACE-DECISIONS.md.

The native app keeps one account and adds Customer, Provider and Owner workspaces, accessible through My workspaces on Home and Profile. The colourful Home layout remains. Workspace preferences do not grant qualification or ownership. Provider availability and future owner contact consent are local preferences. Provider client/reminder/invoice notes and owner viewing/reminder/invoice notes persist per account. Reminders are notes, invoices are drafts, and no notifications or payments are fabricated.

The owner workspace offers a company rental-management enquiry covering rent collection, inspections, repair coordination and premises administration. It is explicitly an external, separately assessed and contracted company service. Enquiries remain LOCAL_PENDING on the device and are not sent to the company. A management contract must verify ownership and representative authority; there is no contract activation endpoint.

Launch charges are waived. Provider Pro, Owner Portfolio and optional Search Pass are inactive interest lists with no confirmed prices or automatic billing. Basic owner chat remains free. Calling requires owner consent and a real calling integration. No paid qualification or purchased organic rating boost. AR navigation is not implemented.

The separate training walkthrough illustrates the confirmed attended-request rule: reserve on technician acceptance; settle once after customer-confirmed arrival. UGX 2,500 is an illustration within the discussed range. Pre-arrival cancellation releases the reservation; post-arrival cancellation preserves attendance and goes to review. Customer confirmation, diagnostic completion, repair quote acceptance and repair completion remain separate. Training uses no marketplace bookings, wallet transactions or real ratings. It cannot approve an account.

Customer booking controls replace the old simulated completion route. Repository rules require actual booking ownership, customer-confirmed arrival and an accepted repair quote for repair completion. Response events persist separately from the latest status. A review needs the actual dispatched technician and customer-confirmed completion; it is unique per confirmed booking, requires valid scores, does not require payment and does not erase an existing dispute. Historical sample reviews remain unconfirmed. Rebooking checks currently eligible providers and does not substitute an unqualified technician. Native discovery remains fail-closed until authoritative snapshots and authentication/synchronisation exist.

The SQLDelight 6.sqm migration upgrades schema 6 to 7, adding workspace records, enquiry/interest/training tables, customer feedback/events and a partial unique index for confirmed reviews. Existing records and historical sample reviews are retained.

The React admin adds Marketplace operations: launch policy, review queue with recorded actors/reasons and fee ledger, management enquiries and plan interest. A reason of at least five characters is required for reviewer release/refund or uphold decisions. Live Bookings no longer offers blind status advancement. Bundled demo booking actions stay local and have no authoritative effect. Missing hosted tables report an error; demo information is not silently substituted for live records.

Concrete hosted changes prepared for review: supabase/migrations/20261013000000_marketplace_workflows.sql. It creates the free-launch policy, provider fee accounts, booking workflow, immutable event and fee ledgers, preferences, interest and enquiry tables. It revokes direct client booking/quote/review writes and provides authenticated, identity-bound RPCs: request_qualified_service, marketplace_action, offer_repair_quote, respond_repair_quote and submit_booking_review. Funding remains trusted-server-only with no public top-up RPC. Paid plans are constrained inactive. Acceptance rechecks qualification, availability, trade, coverage and unreserved balance under a wallet lock; reservation, settlement and refund are transactional and unique. Customer arrival cannot be supplied by a provider or admin. Reviewers cannot manufacture completion. Self-booking is rejected. Nearby ranking uses only confirmed, service-specific booking reviews with a confidence-weighted prior, after qualification, availability, coverage and financial eligibility filters. Approximate coordinates and arrival estimates remain estimates.

No hosted migration, function or admin build has been deployed. Review the SQL before deployment; it intentionally disables legacy direct booking/quote/review writers, so hosted clients must use the new RPCs. Native/cloud synchronisation is not present and the native app does not call these RPCs. Deployment alone does not make the Android preview a live marketplace.

Production dependencies remain: authoritative identity/liveness/trade/title and company-authority registry adapters and credentials; native Supabase authentication and actor-bound synchronisation; payment-provider reconciliation with immutable funding audit/idempotency, actual money and operational refund processing; subscription entitlements/prices after value is proven; owner-consented calling, notifications, maps/arrival tracking, external enquiry delivery and company management operations. No trained model, connected registry, active subscription, guaranteed service warranty, real-time GPS arrival or live payment is claimed. The existing consented verification-extract function remains advisory only. An expiring arrival-code alternative is not implemented. This implementation uses customer-confirmed arrival instead.
